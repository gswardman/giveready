# Google Ad Grants — This-Week Action Plan (WEF / GiveReady)

_Compiled 2026-06-18. Goal: $10,000/month in free Google Search ads driving traffic to the GiveReady directory. This is an enrollment, not a competitive grant — clear the gates and you're in._

---

## The honest summary

Three gates stand between you and the money:
1. **The WEF must be a verified 501(c)(3)** (you have the status; it needs to be *verified* by Google's partner, Goodstack).
2. **The website you send ads to must pass Google's content rules** — this is the only real work, and it's about giveready.org being substantive and secure.
3. **You set up and keep an Ad Grants Google Ads account compliant** (ongoing, light, but real rules).

None of this is hard. Gate 2 is where the week's effort goes.

---

## Step 1 — Enroll in Google for Nonprofits + get verified (start TODAY)

- Go to google.com/nonprofits and request an account **as the WEF**, using the WEF's legal name and EIN.
- You'll be routed to **Goodstack** (formerly Percent) for verification. They confirm: the WEF is an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3), operates not-for-profit, you're an authorized representative, and it's in good standing.
- **Turnaround: 3–5 business days.** Watch for email from `verifications@mail.goodstack.org` (check spam).
- **What you need ready:** WEF EIN, IRS determination letter, and proof you represent the org.

> This is the long pole on the calendar, so kick it off first — the 3–5 day clock runs while you do Step 2.

## Step 2 — Get giveready.org compliant (the real work this week)

Google's website policy is the thing that actually gets nonprofits rejected. Requirements:

- **HTTPS / valid SSL.** The site must be secure. Confirm giveready.org serves over HTTPS with a valid cert.
- **You own the domain** ads point to. ✓ (you do)
- **At least 5 pages of substantive content** — Google reads "substantive" as 300+ words of original, unique text per page. Not placeholder, not boilerplate.
- **Clear mission + what you do**, updated info, and **clear calls to action** (e.g. "find a charity," "donate," "list your nonprofit").
- **No prohibited setup:** the site can't be primarily commercial, can't run Google AdSense/affiliate links on landing pages, and the donate/CTA flow should work.

**Action:** audit giveready.org against the 5-page / 300-word / HTTPS bar. If it's thin, the fastest fix is to flesh out: (1) Home/mission, (2) How it works / x402 for charities, (3) About the WEF + Finn, (4) For nonprofits (how to get listed/receive payments), (5) For donors/agents. I can draft any of these pages this week.

## Step 3 — Activate Ad Grants + build the first campaign

Once verified and the site passes:

- In Google for Nonprofits, **activate Google Ad Grants**. Watch the short required welcome video. Submit the website URL.
- Create the **Google Ads account** under the grant. Build **at least one campaign** with: 2+ ad groups, 2+ ads per ad group, 2+ sitelink extensions, geo-targeting set, conversion tracking on.
- First campaigns to consider: "find a charity for [cause]," "donate to [cause] charity," "how nonprofits accept AI/agent payments." Tie keywords to real GiveReady pages.

## Step 4 — Stay compliant (ongoing, so you don't lose it)

- Maintain a **5%+ click-through rate** each month (the #1 reason grants get suspended).
- **No single-word keywords**, no overly generic ones.
- Keep conversion tracking active; log in regularly.
- Standard max CPC is capped at $2 unless you use Smart Bidding (Maximize Conversions), which removes the cap — use it.
