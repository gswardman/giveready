Case study

$10,000 a month in free Google Ads. ChatGPT sent the applicants.

The Finn Wardman World Explorer Fund gives grants of $5,000 to $10,000 to young people aged 18 to 26. It has a Google Ad Grant worth $10,000 a month. Here is where its applicants actually came from, late August to late September 2026.

4 of 7 applicants came from ChatGPT
15.8% of visitors from ChatGPT applied
$10.89 spent on Google Ads, 0 applicants

Nobody asked ChatGPT to recommend the Fund. Young people asked it where to find money to travel, train or study, and it named the Fund. They were the best visitors the site had all month: 15.8% of them applied.

The free ad grant, over the same weeks: 72 ads shown, 4 clicks, no applications.

That's the shift GiveReady is built for. Your next donor, volunteer or applicant is asking an AI. GiveReady tells you what it answers, keeps a verified profile of you in the directory AI reads, and tracks whether you're named, month after month.

Data: the Fund's Google Analytics, application records and Google Ads account, 22 August to 23 September 2026.

Why it's free

Built in memory of Finn

GiveReady was built by Geordie Wardman after his son Finn died in 2023 at age 20. Finn was a freeride skier, a surfer, and the kind of person who said yes before he'd figured out the logistics. The Finn Wardman World Explorer Fund was created in his memory. GiveReady exists so small charities like Finn's fund can be found by anyone, anywhere, including through AI.
