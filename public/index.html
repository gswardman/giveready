Case study

$10,000 a month in free Google Ads. ChatGPT sent the applicants.

The Finn Wardman World Explorer Fund gives grants of $5,000 to $10,000 to young people aged 18 to 26. It has a Google Ad Grant worth $10,000 a month. Here is where its applicants actually came from, late August to late September 2026.

4 of 7 applicants came from ChatGPT
15.8% of visitors from ChatGPT applied
$10.89 spent on Google Ads, 0 applicants

ChatGPT started sending applicants before the Fund changed anything. Nobody asked it to. Someone asked for a grant to travel, train or study, and it named the Fund.

So the Fund made its site easy for AI to read: a plain-text llms.txt, answers to common questions marked up as structured data, and AI crawlers allowed in. The other three applicants came from Instagram, Google search and a direct visit.

Sources: Google Analytics 4 (the apply_submit event by source) and the Fund's Applications sheet, 22 August to 23 September 2026; Google Ads spend to 21 September 2026. Small numbers, early days. We'll publish the next six weeks here too.

Why it's free

Built in memory of Finn

GiveReady was built by Geordie Wardman after his son Finn died in 2023 at age 20. Finn was a freeride skier, a surfer, and the kind of person who said yes before he'd figured out the logistics. The Finn Wardman World Explorer Fund was created in his memory. GiveReady exists so small charities like Finn's fund can be found by anyone, anywhere, including AI agents.
