# GiveReady / WEF — Grant Targets + Perplexity Brief

_Compiled 2026-06-18. GiveReady = organic donation engine for the Finn Wardman World Explorer Fund (WEF, US 501(c)(3)). Lets charities receive x402 agentic micropayments and learn to use AI to further their missions. Live MVP, currently housed inside TestVentures.net._

---

## The shortlist — ranked by "easy + good fit"

### Tier 1 — apply to these first (low effort, strong fit)

**1. Coinbase Developer Platform / x402 Builder Grants — up to $30K**
GiveReady is literally built on x402. This is a developer/builder grant, not a 501(c)(3) grant, so eligibility is about the build, not the org's budget. Coinbase + Cloudflare are standing up the x402 Foundation (incubated under the Linux Foundation; backers include Stripe, AWS, Google, Visa, Circle, Anthropic). Grants run in cohorts. Fastest path, tightest product fit. As of April 2026 the network had ~69K active agents and $50M in volume, so they're actively funding adoption.

**2. Fast Forward Accelerator — $25K unrestricted + mentorship**
Built specifically for early-stage tech and AI nonprofits. Two hard requirements: a nonprofit business model (WEF ✓) and a working MVP (GiveReady ✓). Three-month program, unrestricted cash, backed by Google.org, the Patrick J. McGovern Foundation, and Bridgespan. **Applications open July 30 and close September 8, 2026.** This one is almost made for you — put it on the calendar now.

**3. Optimism RetroPGF / Gitcoin retro funding — variable (OP tokens)**
Retroactive public-goods funding. You apply *after* shipping, with evidence of adoption and usage — no proposal writing, no milestones, no 501(c)(3) requirement. GiveReady as open onchain donation infrastructure is squarely the kind of thing these rounds fund. Lowest-effort application in the list; the work is already done. Watch Gitcoin for live rounds (Optimism, Base Builder Grants, Celo, Filecoin all run variants).

### Tier 2 — worth doing, slightly more effort

**4. AWS Imagine Grant — up to $200K cash + up to $100K AWS credits**
US 501(c) nonprofits using cloud tech. Three tracks; the Pathfinder–Generative AI award is up to $200K. Caveat: the 2026 window looked like ~May 19–June 5, so it may have **just closed** — confirm whether it's still open or note the 2027 cycle. Would mean running some GiveReady infra on AWS.

**5. Google Ad Grants + software donations — $10K/month in ads, plus discounted/free tools**
Near-automatic for a 501(c)(3): Google Ad Grants ($10K/month in search ads), Microsoft for Nonprofits (Azure/365 credits), Salesforce Power of Us, TechSoup discounts. Not cash, but real budget relief and traffic for the directory. Quick wins once the WEF's 501(c)(3) paperwork is in hand.

### Tier 3 — bigger, slower, mission-aligned (later)

**6. Patrick J. McGovern Foundation — ~$100K–$1M**
AI/data-science for social good; runs AI Fluency and Capacity Building grants. Strong mission fit but a heavier lift and longer cycle.

**7. Microsoft Philanthropies AI for Good — $50K–$500K** / **Google.org — $1–5M.** Competitive, slower.

### Skip for now
- **OpenAI People-First AI Fund** — requires an annual operating budget **over $500K** (and under $10M). The WEF is almost certainly too small today. Revisit once the budget grows.
- **Gates Foundation** — your own read: long shot, far out. Agreed.

## Reality checks before you apply

- Most US tech grants want a **501(c)(3) in good standing** and basic financials/budget. The WEF qualifies on status; have a one-page budget and a clear statement that GiveReady is a WEF program ready.
- The **x402 and RetroPGF routes don't need 501(c)(3) status** — they fund the build/protocol. Those are the fastest because they sidestep the nonprofit-paperwork gate entirely.
- A few funders (OpenAI fund) have **minimum budget floors** that exclude small orgs. Read eligibility before spending time.
- I'm not a lawyer or accountant — before formally housing GiveReady under the WEF for grant purposes, confirm the activity fits the WEF's exempt purpose with a US nonprofit attorney/CPA.
