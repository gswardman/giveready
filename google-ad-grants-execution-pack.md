## Part 1: Goodstack verification (start TODAY, it's the 3–5 day clock)

### Documents to have open before you start

1. **WEF EIN** (the IRS-issued Employer Identification Number on the determination letter).
2. **IRS determination letter** (the 501(c)(3) approval letter, PDF). Goodstack may ask for it directly.
3. **Proof you represent the org.** A WEF email address is ideal (e.g. info@finnwardman.com). Board-listing or an official document naming you as director/officer also works if asked.
4. **The WEF's exact legal name as it appears on the determination letter.** Type it character-for-character. A mismatch here is the single most common cause of verification delay.
5. **The WEF's US registered address** (must match the IRS records).

### The clicks, in order

1. Go to **google.com/nonprofits**. Top right, click **Get started**.
2. Choose the WEF's **country of registration: United States**, accept the terms, click **Next / Continue**.
3. Enter the WEF's details **exactly as on the IRS determination letter**: legal name, EIN, registered address. Do not abbreviate or reorder anything.
4. Add the contact details: your name, your WEF email address, phone. Use the WEF email, not a personal Gmail, so the "you represent the org" check is clean.
5. Submit. Google hands the request to **Goodstack** (formerly Percent) for verification.
6. **Watch your inbox (and spam) for mail from `verifications@mail.goodstack.org`.** Add that address to your safe-senders list now. They often ask for the determination letter or a quick confirmation that you're affiliated with the WEF. Reply same-day to keep the clock short.
7. **Turnaround is typically 3–5 business days.** You can check status at any time inside the Google for Nonprofits request, or via Goodstack's status page if they email you a link.

### While the clock runs

Do the giveready.org pre-submit checklist (Part 4) and stage the campaign (Part 3) so you can build it the day verification clears.

## Part 3: First Search campaign (paste-ready)

Built to the 2026 Ad Grants rules: two ad groups, two responsive search ads each, no single-word keywords, intent-bearing terms only, four account-level sitelinks, specific geo-targeting, conversion-based Smart Bidding to lift the $2 CPC cap. Every keyword maps to a real giveready.org page, and every ad is WEF-mission-relevant.

### Campaign settings

- **Campaign name:** WEF: Youth Charity Discovery
- **Type:** Search (Search Network only; turn OFF Display Network and search partners)
- **Geo-targeting:** United States and United Kingdom. (Your guide content covers US and UK youth charities; donor and tax-deductibility interest is strongest in the US.) Set **Location options → Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations** (not "interested in"). Do not leave it on "All countries"; that breaks compliance.
- **Languages:** English
- **Networks:** Search only
- **Budget:** $329/day (this is the standard Ad Grants daily cap that maps to the $10,000/month grant; Google sets it, you confirm)
- **Bidding:** see "Smart Bidding" below

### Bid strategy / lifting the $2 CPC cap

- The manual $2.00 max-CPC cap only applies to **Maximize Clicks**. Any **conversion-based Smart Bidding** strategy removes it (your ads can then bid $4, $8, $12+ when a conversion looks likely).
- **Set the strategy to: Maximize Conversions.** Requirement: conversion tracking must be live first (see below).
- **Sequencing:** if you have zero conversion data on day one, start on **Maximize Clicks** for ~2 weeks to gather data, then switch to **Maximize Conversions** as soon as you have ~15–30 conversions in 30 days. The day you switch, the cap is gone.

### Conversion tracking (must be live, this is what makes Smart Bidding legal and effective)

Set these up as **Primary** conversions (meaningful actions):

- Newsletter / email sign-up on giveready.org
- Outbound click to a charity's donate button (a donor handoff)
- Any "support / give" form completion

Set these as **Secondary** (category "Other") so they don't pollute Smart Bidding:

- Homepage views and general page views (these must NOT be primary conversions)

Aim for at least 1 meaningful conversion per month (a hard compliance requirement). Keep your real conversion rate in the believable 2–10% band; if conversions track near 100% of clicks, Google flags the setup.
